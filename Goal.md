### Goal



The platform aims to reshape the learning experience by leveraging generative artificial intelligence to transform academic content into narrative-driven, animated educational experiences inspired by the "anime" style. In this framework, scientific concepts and terminology are reimagined as fictional characters; definitions and explanations evolve into immersive worlds and engaging adventures; and processes or relationships between concepts are depicted as interconnected events and interactions. Through this approach, the platform seeks to make learning more enjoyable and interactive, helping students grasp and solidify their knowledge while boosting their interest and motivation—thereby moving beyond traditional educational models that rely exclusively on reading, rote memorization, and the passive reception of information.
After watching the video, students can take a practice assessment—generated based on the original academic material they uploaded—to gauge their level of understanding and retention of the information and concepts covered in the video.

By employing this method, the platform aspires to make the learning process more engaging and appealing, simplify the comprehension of academic subjects and disciplines, and heighten student interest and motivation. Ultimately, this contributes to enriching the educational experience and improving academic performance.

In its current phase, the platform focuses on university students, who often face various educational challenges—such as dense curricula, academic and psychological pressures, and difficulties in grasping certain subjects. Furthermore, some students may lose the joy of learning or experience a decline in motivation—often as a result of constantly comparing themselves to their higher-achieving peers.

Looking ahead, the project aims to expand the platform to reach a broader range of learners, serving as a supportive educational tool across various fields and disciplines, rather than being limited solely to university students.





### Objectives




1.	Developing a mechanism for receiving and processing academic files: Enabling students to upload their academic materials, from which text and information are extracted for further processing.
2.	Analyzing academic content to extract core knowledge: Identifying the scientific concepts, terminology, definitions, processes, and relationships contained within the content.
3.	Transforming academic knowledge into narrative elements: Representing scientific concepts and terms as characters, and converting definitions, processes, and relationships into interconnected narrative worlds, events, and interactions.
4.	Creating a narrative-based educational scenario: Developing a comprehensive script that translates academic content into a cohesive educational story, while ensuring the accuracy and scientific integrity of the information.
5.	Generating animated educational scenes: Converting the script into visual scenes—inspired by an anime style—and compiling them into a short educational film that presents the content through a narrative framework.
6.	Designing an assessment test: Generating evaluation questions based on the original academic material to gauge the student's understanding of the content after watching the film.
7.	Evaluating student responses: Reviewing the student's answers to the test to determine their level of comprehension regarding the academic concepts and information covered in the film.





### Features




1.	Uploading Academic Files: Support for uploading lectures and educational materials for content analysis.
2.	Intelligent Content Analysis: Extracting scientific concepts, terms, definitions, processes, and relationships from the files.
3.	Creative Narrative Transformation: Converting scientific information into interconnected characters, worlds, adventures, and plot events.
4.	Customizable Storytelling Styles: Allowing students to choose a narrative style that suits their preferences—such as fantasy, drama, action, adventure, or comedy.
5.	Maintaining Scientific Accuracy: Ensuring that fictional elements serve to explain scientific concepts without distorting the underlying academic information.
6.	Character and Scene Generation: Creating characters and visual scenes tailored to the educational script.
7.	Interactive Voices and Dialogue: Providing distinct voices for characters, enabling them to engage in dialogue and interact with one another according to their roles within the story, rather than relying on a single narrator reading the lecture.
8.	Production of Animated Educational Films: Transforming scripts, characters, scenes, and dialogue into short educational films inspired by the anime style.
9.	Creation of Interactive Quizzes: Developing assessments based on the original academic material for students to complete after watching the film.
10.	Assessing Comprehension: Analyzing student responses to gauge their level of understanding of the scientific material.
11.	Ease of Use: Providing a clear, simple interface that enables students to navigate and use the platform with ease. 12. Scalability and Extensibility: Designing the system in a way that allows for the future addition of new features and functionalities, in line with user needs and technological advancements.





### Deliverables





1.	Integrated Educational Platform: An interactive interface that allows students to access and utilize the system’s functions.
2.	Academic File Upload and Processing System: A software component that enables students to upload academic files, extract their content, and prepare it for analysis.
3.	Academic Content Analysis System: An AI-driven component that analyzes academic material to extract key concepts, terms, definitions, processes, and fundamental relationships.
4.	Educational Scenario Generation System: A component that transforms knowledge extracted from academic content into coherent narrative scenarios, while maintaining scientific accuracy.
5.	Narrative Style Customization System: A feature allowing students to select the style of their educational film based on their personal preferences and interests—such as fantasy, drama, action, adventure, or comedy.
6.	Educational Characters and Scenes: Visual designs for characters, worlds, and settings that represent scientific concepts and narrative events.
7.	Animated Educational Films: Short, anime-style films produced based on the educational scenarios and the narrative style chosen by the student.
8.	Assessment Generation System: A component that creates practice tests based on the original academic file uploaded by the student, following their viewing of the film.
9.	Student Response Evaluation System: A component that analyzes a student’s test answers to determine their level of understanding and grasp of the academic content.
10.	Technical Project Documentation: Documentation detailing the system’s architecture, components, operational mechanisms, and the technologies used in its development.
